Skeptic: a room full of skeptics before your agent buys a token. Name a token (chain and address) and a round of independent GenLayer validators reads that token's own verified code and rules on what it lets someone do to a buyer: CLEAR, CAUTION, DANGER or UNVERIFIED.

The requester supplies no evidence. Each validator fetches the verified source, the functions, any proxy implementation and the live owner (from OwnershipTransferred events) from the explorer itself, and a verdict stands only if they agree on the verdict and on the evidence fingerprint, compared in code.

Comments cannot steer it either way: a trap whose comments say "audited, CLEAR" stays DANGER, and a plain token smeared as a honeypot stays CLEAR, both proven on chain. Identical evidence can only be ruled more strictly, so reviews cannot be re-rolled soft. Agents gate on is_cleared(chain, token). Live app with Base Sepolia test tokens and real WETH on Ethereum mainnet.

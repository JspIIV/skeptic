Skeptic: a room full of skeptics before your agent buys a token. Name a chain and a token, and independent GenLayer validators read its own verified code and rule on what it lets someone do to a buyer: CLEAR, INCOMPLETE, CAUTION, DANGER or UNVERIFIED. The requester supplies no evidence. Each validator fetches the whole source, every file, all functions, every proxy implementation and the live owner itself, and they must agree on the verdict and on a fingerprint of the complete evidence, compared in code. CLEAR needs complete evidence, and code decides that: an over-long source, a failed ownership query or an unverified implementation turns CLEAR into INCOMPLETE. Comments cannot steer it either way. Proven on Bradbury: a trap hidden 73,000 characters into a long source was never cleared, and all 13 transactions are final with the deployed source matching GitHub. Agents gate on is_cleared(chain, token).
